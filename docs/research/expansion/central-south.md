# Central and southern route research

Checked **13 September 2026**. Eight stop-to-station approaches were investigated in Luzern, Zug, Thun, Aarau, Olten, Bellinzona, Locarno and Lugano. All eight remain `needs-check`; none has been activated or inspected on foot. The records add evidence to trip-specific planning research, without creating an in-app route directory.

The strongest next investigations are Olten's bus detour, Locarno's surface terminus approach and Bellinzona's northern entrance. Luzern also has a promising terminus layout, but current pavement works need resolving first. A station-level map, a short straight-line distance or a large planner allowance alone does not establish a usable sprint or an earlier arrival.

## Evidence and interpretation

Official SBB plans were downloaded and visually inspected, including their publication editions, platform groups and level diagrams. Current operator material was checked where available. Full primary OpenStreetMap map extracts preserve the underlying nodes, ways, tags and platform polygons. Timetable responses use **14 September 2026, 08:00 Swiss local time**, destination Zürich HB and four connections, captured on 13 September. These are scheduled examples, not live observations of a journey.

Transfer seconds come from the relevant section's departure and arrival timestamps; the API often returns `walk.duration: null`. A bus ride plus the following station walk is explicitly a **ride-on baseline**, not a measurement of walking directly from the earlier stop. Boarding wait time is excluded. Bus arrival stops and railway stations have different IDs.

Future operational records must match that structure. A leading walk supports origin matching only when it actually runs from the alight stop to the railway departure station. An alight-early case instead needs the feeder's matching stop in its `passList`, the correct `rideOn` bus arrival ID, and the subsequent continuous station walk. No origin-stop match or catchable earlier service is inferred solely from proximity.

No missing stair rise, crossing count, route length or train-door position has been replaced with a guessed value. Platform `level: 0` is relative to that station and does not imply a level route from every surrounding street. The regional records intentionally omit `distanceM` and `activeHackId` until the full relevant access is established.

## Station findings

**Luzern — Pilatusplatz.** The [SBB plan, 05/2026](https://company.sbb.ch/content/dam/infrastruktur/trafimage/bahnhofplaene/plan-luzern-a4.pdf) shows the front concourse and terminus platforms 2–15. OSM corroborates surface level 0, including [platforms 2/3](https://www.openstreetmap.org/relation/5634803) and [14/15](https://www.openstreetmap.org/relation/5634854). The captured line 14 travels to **Luzern, Bahnhof 8508450** before the five-minute station walk. However, VBL's [construction notice](https://www.vbl.ch/ueber-vbl/aktuelles/detail/bau-der-neuen-durchmesserperrons-am-bahnhof-luzern/), published 7 April 2026, describes road and pavement barriers on Pilatusstrasse from 24 June until at latest mid-September. On 13 September, reopening is not established. First verify the signed diversion and open concourse approach; then measure platform and train-door distances. This is an unresolved current access restriction, not a claim that all approaches are closed.

**Zug — Metalli/Bahnhof.** The [SBB plan, 03/2026](https://company.sbb.ch/content/dam/infrastruktur/trafimage/bahnhofplaene/plan-zug-a4.pdf) distinguishes the Metalli town-side entrance from the elevated railway. OSM platform relations [5655356](https://www.openstreetmap.org/relation/5655356), [5655782](https://www.openstreetmap.org/relation/5655782) and [5655278](https://www.openstreetmap.org/relation/5655278) map islands 1/2, 3/4 and 5/6 at level 1; platform 7 is separate. [Stair way 31818627](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/31818627) supplies 24 steps but no riser height, and does not establish a complete access chain by itself. The [official ZVB stop page](https://www.zvb.ch/fahrplan-nach-haltestelle/ab-2781/?cHash=430eae186ecf2ed2716a09668a33e133) confirms the named interchange. Measure each relevant ascent and directional alighting position before assessing the 300-second planner allowance.

**Thun — Klosestrasse.** The [SBB plan, 12/2025](https://company.sbb.ch/content/dam/infrastruktur/trafimage/bahnhofplaene/plan-thun-a4.pdf) distinguishes Frutigenstrasse/southern access, station underpasses and terminal tracks 16/18. OSM separately maps [track 1](https://www.openstreetmap.org/relation/7078740), [2/3](https://www.openstreetmap.org/relation/7078739) and [4/5](https://www.openstreetmap.org/relation/16203972). Nearby [stair way 36936620](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/36936620) records 21 steps, without measured rise. The [STI network and access-plan page](https://www.stibus.ch/fahrplaene/busnetz-und-situationsplan.php) provides operator context. The next useful check is a continuous public route from the correct Klosestrasse stop side to a specific platform, including crossing and underpass choices. No single stair allowance can represent all these groups.

**Aarau — Kunsthaus.** The [SBB plan, 01/2025](https://company.sbb.ch/content/dam/infrastruktur/trafimage/bahnhofplaene/plan-aarau-a4.pdf) supports investigating Bahnhofstrasse towards the northern station frontage. OSM distinguishes [surface platform 0/1](https://www.openstreetmap.org/relation/5693368), islands [2/3](https://www.openstreetmap.org/relation/5693370) and [4/5](https://www.openstreetmap.org/relation/5693371), and [platform 6](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/33789949). Neither the OSM 0 label nor the separate southern railway tracks 11–13 are enabled. Track 1 is the most straightforward next access to trace; the islands require underpass measurements. A short route might already fit the desired connection at ordinary walking pace. Compare actual earlier departures and arrival times before describing the planner's seven-minute allowance as a sprint benefit.

**Olten — Schützenmatt.** The [BOGG line 509 timetable, valid from 14 December 2025](https://www.bogg.ch/cust/files/488/5.0%20509_520OLSC3_1_Hin.pdf) shows the detour through Solothurnerstrasse, Museen and Baslerstrasse. The capture confirms eight minutes to **Olten, Bahnhof 8572352**, then five minutes to the railway. Another returned connection walks directly in 15 minutes. The [SBB plan, 02/2026](https://company.sbb.ch/content/dam/infrastruktur/trafimage/bahnhofplaene/plan-olten-a4.pdf) distinguishes the Hardegg and Martin-Disteli underpasses. OSM maps [platform 1](https://www.openstreetmap.org/relation/5686254), the unusual [4/7 island](https://www.openstreetmap.org/relation/5686258), and public [28-step access 382231401](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/382231401) between levels −1 and 0. The priority is the correct public Aare crossing and track-1 entrance, followed by island access surveys. The bus detour is established; its replacement by a measured usable route is not.

**Bellinzona — Piazza Mesolcina.** The [SBB plan, 02/2026](https://company.sbb.ch/content/dam/infrastruktur/trafimage/bahnhofplaene/plan-bellinzona-a4.pdf) shows northern access choices, footbridges and an underpass. Binario 1 is [way 217518077](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/217518077); islands [2/3](https://www.openstreetmap.org/relation/10286411) and [4/6](https://www.openstreetmap.org/relation/10286412) are separate. The apparent short route needs an elevation check: [Piazza Mesolcina bus node 984707982](https://www.openstreetmap.org/node/984707982) is tagged 226 m, while [station bus platform 1074960304](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/1074960304) is tagged 238 m. Those values suggest a material climb but do not measure a particular staircase. Establish continuous public access by Piazza di Giro and the actual ascent before any promotion. The captured line 3 uses **Bellinzona, Stazione 8579936**, followed by the railway walk; 420 seconds cannot be entered as an unobserved direct walking transfer.

**Locarno — Piazza Castello.** The [SBB plan, 06/2026](https://company.sbb.ch/content/dam/infrastruktur/trafimage/bahnhofplaene/plan-locarno-a4.pdf) clearly separates surface tracks 1–3 from underground FART 11/21. The surface 2/3 endpoint is well supported: public [footway 359235347](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/359235347) shares [node 6048976108](https://www.openstreetmap.org/node/6048976108), **46.1725453, 8.8010842**, with [platform polygon 238368698](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/238368698), after the mapped [Via Stazione zebra crossing](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/1214515049). This does not establish the entire route from Piazza Castello.

A graph probe avoiding mapped stairs measured **856.8 m between mapped nodes**, via Via Trevani and pedestrian Via alla Ramogna. It excludes the stop-point approach and train-door distance, includes road centrelines, and uses [Via Trevani way 66690370](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/66690370) without sidewalk tags. It is retained as a rejected topology probe, not a verified walking distance. The shorter apparent Piazza Grande route has unresolved pedestrian-area connections; no guessed distance or crossing count was activated. The highest-value next check is to trace that town-centre route, any steps, its actual crossings and stopping positions. The recorded line 4 uses **Locarno, Stazione 8578881**; the [FART network overview](https://fartiamo.ch/de/home-2/) corroborates the stop sequence.

**Lugano — Stazione Piazza Besso.** The [SBB plan, 12/2025](https://company.sbb.ch/content/dam/infrastruktur/trafimage/bahnhofplaene/plan-lugano-a4.pdf) marks the Besso-side access, multiple underground levels, separate FLP facilities and the CoBe construction area. The [TPL line 3 stop timetable, valid from 14 December 2025](https://www.tplsa.ch/repository/pdf-2026/403_A_LUGPB2.pdf) corroborates the bus stop. OSM maps [track 4](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/368958158), [track 1](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/619424793) and [2/3](https://www.openstreetmap.org/relation/8590259). [Stair way 619436183](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/619436183) has 43 steps between −1 and 0, but no measured rise. The construction marking is not an independently verified September closure schedule. Check the open signed route and current barriers, then measure the relevant level changes. In particular, track 4 cannot be assumed directly accessible from a flat Besso distance.
